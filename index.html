SPACEstamp the tail for a hard pivot
SPACE ×2+ a turn direction: snap. The steeper the lip and the harder you are climbing at it, the further it comes round — and out on the flats it is a speed check, to let the section catch up
↑↑pump — lunge forward and drive off the rising face for speed
↓↓holdbrake. Weight goes back over the fins; keep it held to bury the rail and stall a section open, release to let go

Standing forward planes the board flat: glide, speed, long drawn rail arcs. Standing back sinks the tail — slower, but it puts you over the fins. You can only stamp a tail you're standing near, so walk back before you commit to a turn, and walk forward to run.

It's a right, so the wall is on your right and the flats are on your left. Stay in the pocket: outrun it and you're stranded on a dead shoulder with nothing to score, sit behind it and the foam eats you. Read the wall ahead — a fast section wants a high, straight, driving line, a fat section wants you to brake and carve back into the power.
